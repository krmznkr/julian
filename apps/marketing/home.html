A year view for Google Calendar

Your whole year on one screen.

Julian lays out twelve months side by side so trips, projects, and holidays read as shapes instead of scattered boxes. It runs in your browser and talks to Google directly.

Try the live demo Read the source

The demo is the real app on sample data. Connect your Google Calendar from there when you are ready.

What it does

  • Twelve months, one grid

    Multi-day events become bars across the days they cover, in lanes that never overlap.

  • Find anything

    Press / to filter the year by text; matches stay highlighted in the grid.

  • Plan by moving things

    Drag an all-day event to another day, or move it with the keyboard. Changes go straight to Google Calendar.

  • Keyboard first, touch friendly

    Arrow keys walk the grid, a command palette does the rest, and it works on a phone.

  • Prints on one page

    Print the year as a single landscape sheet for the wall or the fridge.

  • No server-side data

    Your calendar goes from Google to your browser. Julian has no database.
