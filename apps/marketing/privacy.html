Privacy

Julian is a browser app. It reads and changes your Google Calendar from your browser, on your behalf, and keeps nothing about your calendar on a server.

What Julian asks Google for

Signing in asks for these Google permissions and nothing else:

Julian only changes events when you create, rename, move, or delete one. It never reads your email, contacts, or files.

Where your data lives

  • Calendar events and tasks are fetched by your browser straight from Google's APIs and kept in memory while the page is open. Julian has no database and stores no calendar data on any server.
  • Your Google access and refresh tokens are kept in your browser's local storage on this device. Signing out removes them; you can also revoke access any time in your Google account settings.
  • Display preferences (theme, hidden calendars, sidebar state) are kept in local storage too. A cookie named julian_session records only that this browser has signed in, so the home page can open your calendar directly. It holds no token and is cleared when you sign out.

Hosting

Julian is served by Cloudflare Workers. To sign in, the Worker passes the one-time authorization code and refresh requests to Google with the app's client secret and returns Google's answer to your browser without storing it. Cloudflare processes request metadata such as IP addresses to deliver the site and to rate-limit abuse. Julian has no ads.

Errors and traffic analytics

On the production site, Sentry receives minimized browser error reports and a 5% sample of performance traces. Error text is replaced with a fixed label; reports retain app stack locations and timing information to help fix bugs. PostHog receives page views and page leaves in its EU region. Analytics use temporary in-memory identifiers, with no analytics cookies, persistent identifiers, or person profiles, and respect Do Not Track.

Neither service receives your calendar events, task or calendar titles, email address, Google tokens, browser preferences, page text, request bodies, cookies, or headers from Julian. URLs are reduced to static page categories without query strings or fragments. There is no session replay, automatic click collection, advertising profiling, or feature flag collection. The services process network metadata such as IP addresses to receive requests. Development, nightly and preview sites do not send these reports or analytics.
